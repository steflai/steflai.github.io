Title:
Attitudes towards Chinese food of young Hong Kong Chinese migrants in
London: a study of home and identity
ii
© 2026 steflai.github.io - All Rights Reserved.
ABSTRACT
In this dissertation, I will consider how food relates to migrants’ identity
and sense of home. The following questions will be asked: How does food create
a sense of home? An identity? Or recreate memories? How do the consumption,
preparation and appreciation of food show one’s sense of cosmopolitanism at
home and abroad?
Through in-depth interviews and participation observation during meals
and cooking I will explore how the young Hong Kong Chinese migrants in
London, through their actions, choices and sensory appreciation of Chinese food,
create a new cosmopolitan migrant identity and concept of home. This study of a
specific migrant group in London will hopefully contribute a fresh perspective on
a little-studied migrant population in the UK and to the existing body of
literature on food and migration.
